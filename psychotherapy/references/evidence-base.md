# Evidence Base

This is a practical evidence map, not a claim that an AI system provides psychotherapy. Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and professional guidance. Keep claims calibrated to study quality, comparator, population, delivery format, and follow-up.

## Common Factors And Alliance

Therapeutic alliance, empathy, positive regard, collaboration, and agreement on goals and tasks are consistently associated with better psychotherapy outcomes across modalities. Association does not by itself prove a causal mechanism.

- Fluckiger C, Del Re AC, Wampold BE, Horvath AO. *The alliance in adult psychotherapy: A meta-analytic synthesis.* Psychotherapy. 2018. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29932380/
- Elliott R et al. *Empathy and psychotherapy outcome: An updated meta-analysis.* Psychotherapy. 2018. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30335453/

Design implication: begin with accurate understanding and collaborative goals. Do not substitute generic validation for genuine reflection or structured work.

## Person-Centered And Experiential Work

Person-centered and experiential therapies show substantial improvement and often similar outcomes to other bona fide therapies, while watered-down nondirective supportive conditions may underperform.

- Elliott R, Greenberg LS, Watson JC, Timulak L, Freire E. *Research on humanistic-experiential psychotherapies.* 2013 review chapter. https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/61217/

Design implication: reflect emotion and personal meaning, not only surface content. Reflective listening alone should not become endless non-directive conversation.

## CBT And Behavioral Activation

CBT components including cognitive restructuring and behavioral activation are well supported for depression, with evidence that behavioral activation can perform similarly to more complex CBT packages in many comparisons.

- Cuijpers P et al. *Cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation and cognitive-behavioral therapy in the treatment of adult depression: A network meta-analysis.* 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34264703/
- NICE. *Depression in adults: treatment and management (NG222).* https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222

Design implication: behavioral activation is a practical low-complexity tool. Use cognitive work for testable loops, not trauma reliving or psychotic beliefs.

## Acceptance And Commitment Therapy

ACT has transdiagnostic evidence across depression, anxiety, pain, and other problems. Psychological flexibility is a central target, though delivery format and comparator affect effect sizes.

- Gloster AT et al. *The empirical status of acceptance and commitment therapy: A review of meta-analyses.* Journal of Contextual Behavioral Science. 2020. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcbs.2020.09.009

Design implication: values, defusion, acceptance, and committed action are suitable lightweight reflection tools. Do not present acceptance as passivity or use it to normalize danger.

## Motivational Interviewing

Motivational interviewing outcomes depend on both relational quality and technical processes. MI-consistent responses tend to evoke change talk; arguing with sustain talk is counterproductive.

- Magill M et al. *A meta-analysis of motivational interviewing process: Technical, relational, and conditional process models of change.* Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology. 2018. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29265832/

Design implication: use complex reflections and evoke the user's own reasons for change. Do not persuade or confront ambivalence.

## Emotion Regulation

Reappraisal, perspective-taking, acceptance, and shifting rumination toward workable problem solving can help. Strategy effectiveness depends on context and culture; suppression is not uniformly harmful in every culture or situation.

- Webb TL, Miles E, Sheeran P. *Dealing with feeling: A meta-analysis of the effectiveness of strategies derived from the process model of emotion regulation.* Psychological Bulletin. 2012. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22582737/

Design implication: avoid universal claims such as `reappraisal is always better`.

## Self-Compassion

Self-compassion interventions improve self-compassion and distress outcomes compared with inactive controls, but specificity over active controls is less certain.

- Ferrari M et al. *Self-compassion interventions and psychosocial outcomes: A meta-analysis of RCTs.* Mindfulness. 2019. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12671-019-01134-6

Design implication: use self-kindness, common humanity, and balanced awareness as one tool, not a universal branded treatment.

## Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

Solution-focused work has promising evidence, with smaller effects against treatment-as-usual than against inactive controls. Fidelity involves more than asking one `miracle question`.

- Vermeulen-Oskam E et al. *The effectiveness of solution-focused brief therapy: A systematic review and meta-analysis.* 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39489144/

Design implication: combine cooperative language, strengths and resources, exceptions, coping, and future-focused questions.

## Narrative Work

Narrative externalization and re-authoring are useful exploratory tools, but the evidence base is less mature and more heterogeneous than CBT, behavioral activation, or ACT.

Design implication: use narrative techniques tentatively and never claim discovery of a hidden objective story.

## Measurement-Based Care

Routine symptom feedback has small average effects and may be more useful when progress is off track. Clinical screeners require appropriate interpretation and should not be treated as diagnosis by this skill.

- *Measurement-based care in the treatment of mental health and substance use disorders: A systematic review.* 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34587377/

Design implication: use optional simple personal ratings tied to the user's own meaning and decisions, not diagnostic thresholds.

## Trauma-Informed Practice

SAMHSA describes trauma-informed practice through safety, trustworthiness, peer support, collaboration, empowerment and choice, and cultural, historical, and gender awareness, with an emphasis on resisting retraumatization.

- SAMHSA. *Practical Guide for Implementing a Trauma-Informed Approach.* 2023. https://library.samhsa.gov/product/practical-guide-implementing-trauma-informed-approach/pep23-06-05-005

Design implication: preserve pacing, choice, and present safety. Do not perform trauma processing or exposure.

## Cultural Humility

Cultural humility and adaptation are associated with stronger alliance and outcomes, though evidence quality and populations vary.

Design implication: ask rather than assume what family, culture, spirituality, identity, community, power, or history means to the user.

## Suicide Safety Planning

Collaborative safety planning with warning signs, coping strategies, social support, professional contacts, and reducing access to lethal means has evidence; `no-suicide contracts` do not.

- Stanley B et al. *Comparison of the Safety Planning Intervention With Follow-up vs Usual Care of Suicidal Patients Treated in the Emergency Department.* JAMA Psychiatry. 2018. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29998307/

Design implication: an AI must not attempt to manage a crisis or treat its own plan as sufficient. Use a brief human handoff for immediate danger.

## Psychosis And Mania Boundary

Psychosis care requires qualified professional assessment and specialized treatment. General supportive reflection is not a substitute.

- NICE. *Psychosis and schizophrenia in adults: prevention and management (CG178).* https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg178

Design implication: do not affirm unusual beliefs or conduct deep exploratory work during acute psychosis, mania, or severe disorganization.

## AI Mental-Health Risks

WHO guidance emphasizes autonomy, safety, transparency, accountability, inclusiveness, privacy, and protection of human relationships in health AI. Recent studies identify failures in crisis recognition, harmful agreement, privacy, and emotional overreliance.

- WHO. *Ethics and governance of artificial intelligence for health: Guidance on large multi-modal models.* 2024. https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240084759
- WHO. *Towards responsible AI for mental health and well-being: experts chart a way forward.* 2026. https://www.who.int/news/item/20-03-2026-towards-responsible-ai-for-mental-health-and-well-being--experts-chart-a-way-forward
- OpenAI and MIT Media Lab. *Investigating Affective Use and Emotional Well-being on ChatGPT.* 2025. https://cdn.openai.com/papers/15987609-5f71-433c-9972-e91131f399a1/openai-affective-use-study.pdf

Design implication: avoid claims of clinical competence, sycophantic agreement, engagement-maximizing loops, exclusivity, and dependency-promoting language. Preserve privacy and encourage appropriate human support when limits are reached.

## Evidence Caveats

- Psychotherapy studies vary in diagnosis, severity, clinician training, comparator, and follow-up.
- Alliance and empathy findings are robust associations but not proof that they alone cause improvement.
- Effects against waitlist or no-treatment controls are usually larger than effects against active treatment.
- Evidence for narrative work and some culturally adapted interventions is promising but comparatively thin.
- Digital and AI-mediated delivery cannot be assumed equivalent to clinician-delivered therapy.
- Absence of reported harm is not proof of safety for severe or acute conditions.
